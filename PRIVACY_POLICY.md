## Information Collection and Use

**Microphone Access**
To function as an instrument tuner, Notefy requires access to your device's microphone.

- **How it is used:** The microphone data is used solely to calculate frequency pitch in real-time.
- **Data Handling:** All audio processing is done **locally on your device** using a native C++ audio engine.
- **No Recording:** Audio data is never recorded, saved to storage, or transmitted to any server. The data is discarded immediately after the pitch is calculated.

**Personal Data**
I do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). The app functions entirely offline.

## Ads and Tracking
